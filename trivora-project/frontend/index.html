Ask Trivora ↗
How much does a website cost?

Website development starts from KSh 30,000. The final quote depends on the number of pages, design needs, content, features and any integrations your project requires.

What do you need from me to get started?

Usually, a short description of your business, the audience you want to reach, your goals, examples you like, and any content or brand assets you already have. We can help clarify the requirements together.

Will my website work on phones and tablets?

Responsive design is part of our website approach. We plan layouts to adapt to mobile, tablet and desktop screens, then test the key pages and interactions across common screen sizes.

Can you connect my existing business systems?

Yes, where the systems provide suitable integration options. We can assess APIs, databases and workflow requirements first, then recommend a practical way to connect the tools and move information reliably.

Do you build web applications and mobile apps?

We offer web and mobile application planning and development, including portals, dashboards and custom business tools. The technology and scope are selected around the use case and your budget.

How long will my project take?

Timelines depend on the project's size, content readiness, integrations and review cycles. Once we understand the requirements, we can agree on a realistic schedule and milestones before work begins.

Can you support my website after launch?

Yes. Maintenance and support can include troubleshooting, updates, performance improvements and feature changes. We can discuss the level of ongoing support that fits your product.
